Questions

Before you join

When will Pace launch?

We're finishing testing now. The Android app comes first on Google Play, with iPhone after. Pace works in any country and currency. Everyone on the waitlist hears first, with one message, no newsletter.

How is this different from a budgeting app?

Most budgeting apps show a monthly total and warn you when it's used up, usually near the end of the month. Pace gives you a daily limit, so you know on the day itself, and it re-spreads any overspend across the days left.

Does Pace connect to my bank account?

No. You enter rough amounts for income and bills, and add spending by typing it or sharing a payment screen from your UPI app. We never ask for bank logins, PINs or card details.

Will it cost anything?

Pace is free while it's in early access, including every Plus feature. People on the waitlist get early access first.

What do you do with my email or number?

We use it only to tell you when Pace is ready to download. We don't share it or sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
